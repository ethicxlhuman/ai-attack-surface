# Related projects

Other maintained resources that cover parts of this space. This repository differs by organising attacks around **the layers of a local or agentic stack** and recording **a dated origin plus a dated history for each technique**. Checked 2026-11-21.

| Project | What it is | Organised by |
|---|---|---|
| [MITRE ATLAS](https://atlas.mitre.org/) | Knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques against AI systems, with case studies | ATT&CK-style tactics |
| [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/) | Ranked risk list for LLM applications | Risk category |
| [OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications](https://genai.owasp.org/) | Ranked risk list for agentic systems | Risk category |
| [OWASP MCP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-mcp-top-10/) | Ranked risk list for the Model Context Protocol | Risk category |
| [NIST AI 100-2 / SP 2400-102 (adversarial ML taxonomy)](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ai/100/2/e2025/final) | Taxonomy and terminology of attacks on ML systems | Lifecycle stage and attacker goal |
| [martinholovsky/awesome-llm-attacks](https://github.com/martinholovsky/awesome-llm-attacks) | Curated list of LLM attack research mapped to OWASP and ATLAS | Attack group |
| [manojrammurthy/awesome-llm-security](https://github.com/manojrammurthy/awesome-llm-security) | Incident-first list with a 2023-2026 timeline | Incident |
| [tachyonic-sh/taxonomy](https://github.com/tachyonic-sh/taxonomy) | Taxonomy of AI attack vectors | Attack vector |
| [kagnlp/Awesome-Agentic-Security](https://github.com/kagnlp/Awesome-Agentic-Security) | Paper list accompanying an agentic-security survey | Survey taxonomy |

If you maintain something similar, open an issue and we will add it.
